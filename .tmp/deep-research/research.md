# Research: Engineering theses and bachelor degree dissertations describing e-commerce projects - examples of well-written technical theses, both English and Polish (praca inzynierska sklep internetowy ecommerce przyklad)

*12 sources consulted — 2026-06-29 08:55 — thorough depth*

## TL;DR
The 12 sources retrieved provide **no usable examples of well-written engineering or bachelor theses on e-commerce projects** in either English or Polish. The most structurally relevant source is Poland's national thesis repository (ORPPD/POL-on), which holds over 3.3 million diploma works but is login-gated and yields no extractable content. Researchers must access institutional repositories directly with authenticated credentials.

---

## Key Findings

1. **Poland's national thesis repository (ORPPD) is the correct starting point** but is inaccessible without institutional credentials. It contains 3,344,308 deposited diploma works covering theses defended before October 1, 2019; works after that date are in a separate system within POL-on 2.0. Searching terms like "sklep internetowy" or "e-commerce" within the system would be required to locate relevant examples. (https://polon.nauka.gov.pl/orpd/login)

2. **No actual thesis examples were retrieved from any source.** Every source either failed to load (Scribd ×2, SlideShare), was a commercial writing-service sample with no original research, a CV template site, a framework homepage, or a programming course — none provided a downloadable or readable engineering/bachelor thesis on an e-commerce project. (All 12 sources)

3. **Engineering theses (prace inżynierskie) in Poland are structurally distinct**: the practical/research component carries more weight than the theoretical section, which should be concise. The practical part typically involves system design, calculations, or analysis of a functioning device/system. This structural norm is relevant when evaluating what a "well-written" example should contain. (https://magisterna5.pl/praca-inzynierska-jak-napisac/)

4. **The only GitHub-hosted student engineering project retrieved** (Lamar University RecCenter) demonstrates a layered .NET architecture (BAL/DAL/Entity/UI layers), ASP.NET/C#, form authentication, and HCI principles — and includes a full Word document thesis. However, it is a recreational center management system, **not** an e-commerce project, limiting its direct relevance. (https://github.com/ojusave/RecCenter)

5. **Core e-commerce technical components documented in sources** include: electronic product presentation, online order-taking, automated account inquiries, online payment handling, and inventory management. Payment systems operate via three models: debit/credit card, electronic funds (digital cheque), and digital cash. Security standards cited include SET (adopted by Visa, MasterCard, AmEx) and BIPS. These represent the minimum technical scope a credible e-commerce thesis should address. (https://www.mastersthesiswriting.com/blog/sample-theses/e-commerce-thesis.html)

6. **Modern e-commerce thesis technology stacks** relevant to engineering projects include React/Next.js for frontend, Node.js/REST APIs for backend, MongoDB for databases, and TypeScript — as evidenced by the University of Helsinki's Full Stack Open curriculum covering these exact domains. Next.js is positioned as a production-grade React framework used in large-scale web applications. These technologies would be appropriate for a contemporary praca inżynierska on a sklep internetowy. (https://fullstackopen.com/en/, https://nextjs.org/)

7. **Multiple high-relevance sources were completely inaccessible**: SlideShare (JavaScript rendering block), Scribd ×2 (paywall/rendering failure), and YUMPU (truncated content). The Scribd document titled "e-Commerce Thesis Title" (ID: 713093508) and the SlideShare "e-commerce thesis" presentation are the most likely to have contained directly relevant content had they loaded. (https://www.scribd.com/document/713093508/e-Commerce-Thesis-Title, https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/e-commerce-thesis/113467054)

## Contradictions & Caveats

- **No source contradicts another** because almost no sources contain substantive, overlapping claims about e-commerce thesis examples specifically.
- **Critical caveat — source set failure**: The majority of sources (8/12) are either entirely irrelevant (CV site, framework homepage, programming course) or inaccessible (Scribd ×2, SlideShare, YUMPU). This source set is **not fit for purpose** for the stated research question.
- **Commercial bias**: Three sources (mastersthesiswriting.com, magisterna5.pl, milople.com) are commercial services with financial incentives to appear authoritative on thesis writing; their claims about thesis structure should not be treated as academic guidance.
- **Temporal anomaly**: The Milople blog source is dated April 6, 2026 — a future date — and cites "Anchor Group 2026" statistics, indicating AI-generated or fabricated content. Its statistics (e.g., "$6.4 trillion e-commerce sales by 2026," "89% of retailers using AI") should be treated as **unverified**.
- **ORPPD split**: Theses defended after October 1, 2019 are in a different system than ORPPD, meaning researchers must check both systems to cover the full range of recent Polish engineering theses.

## Recommendation

**This source set cannot answer the research question.** To find actual examples of well-written engineering/bachelor theses on e-commerce projects, researchers should:

1. **Access ORPPD directly** at polon.nauka.gov.pl with institutional credentials and search "sklep internetowy" or "e-commerce"
2. **Check open institutional repositories** of Polish technical universities: Repozytorium PW (Warsaw University of Technology), AGH, Politechnika Gdańska — many are publicly searchable
3. **Search BASE (base-search.net)** or **DART-Europe** for English-language e-commerce engineering dissertations
4. **Try ProQuest Dissertations & Theses** for peer-reviewed English examples with full methodology chapters

The RecCenter GitHub project (source 3) is the only source providing a structural template for an engineering thesis with layered architecture documentation, despite being off-topic for e-commerce specifically.
